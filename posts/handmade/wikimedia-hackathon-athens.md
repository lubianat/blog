<p>Thank you, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/auregann" target="_blank">Léa Lacroix</a>, for the
coordination! So grateful to join my first international Wiki event and my first Wikimedia Hackathon,
and it could not have been better!</p>

<p>Also very grateful for the scholarship by the Wikimedia Foundation. Lots of learning and lots of fun; I
am sure the experience will be a catalyzer for my technical contributions to the open knowledge
ecosystem.</p>

<p>And, of course, extremely glad to meet such amazing people from all the globe that are making sure
Wikipedia, Wikidata (and more) stay running, stay open, and stay fun. So happy to be able to play a role
in this ecosystem and be a part of the growth of wikitech in LatAm (with Wiki Movimento Brasil, always).
</p>

<p>On the tech side, I'd like to single out a simple contribution: a tool to connect iNaturalist
observations to the Wiki ecosystem, now running at <a href="https://inat2wiki.toolforge.org"
target="_blank">inat2wiki.toolforge.org</a>. But this small contribution is even smaller when
compared to all the intangibles, the connections, and the shared experiences.</p>

<p>Wonderful event and wonderful city. Just thanks!</p>

<p>Edit: I joined as a participant/photographing volunteer, a rewarding experience. It was great to see that
<a href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:Contributions&end=2023-05-27&namespace=all&newOnly=1&start=2023-05-01&tagfilter=&target=TiagoLubiana&offset=&limit=500"
target="_blank"> the pictures I took there</a> were appreciated and later reused by fellow
Wikimedians, such as <a href="https://taavi.wtf/posts/wikimedia-hackathon-athens-2023/" target="_blank">
Taavi on his conference
report</a>.
</p>
